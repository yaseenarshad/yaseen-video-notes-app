# ❝ Pull quotes — a TL;DR without rewriting

**What it does.** A **❝ Quotes** tab beside Chapters / Notes. For each chapter (or each ~10-minute window when the
video has no chapters) it shows the 1–2 lines the AI picked as that part's pull quotes — the speakers' exact words,
never a summary — with the chapter title and its time. The part the video is in now is marked. A **Read mode** shows
only this list, large, as a page you can scroll: the whole episode's best lines in a few minutes of reading.

## How to try it

1. Open an analyzed video (🧪 Analyze first if needed).
2. Click the **❝ Quotes** tab in the side panel (under the video).
   - Click a quote → the video jumps there. Hover a quote → its words are marked in the transcript.
   - Click a chapter title → jump to the start of that chapter.
3. Click **📖 Read mode** (or press **⇧Q**):
   - scroll, or `J` / `K` to step through the quotes (the current one is outlined);
   - `Enter` or a click on a quote → close Read mode and play from that line;
   - the chapter you are in now says **YOU ARE HERE**;
   - `Esc` (or ⇧Q, or ✕ Close) closes it.

## What to judge it on

- Reading only the quotes for a 1–2 h episode: does it tell you whether (and where) to watch?
- Are the picks the lines you would have underlined? Too long, too short, cut mid-sentence?
- One-to-two per chapter: right density? (The header shows "~N min read".)
- Read mode vs the panel tab: which one do you reach for?

## Known limits

- Needs a Labs analysis; quotes are only as good as its `pullQuotes`.
- Group titles come from the video's chapters (YouTube's, not the AI's); without chapters the parts are
  "0:00–10:00" windows.
- Reading time is the quotes' word count at 250 words/min — for long, chapter-heavy videos it is more than a minute.
- Read mode hides the video; playback keeps going (audio) until you pause or close it.
