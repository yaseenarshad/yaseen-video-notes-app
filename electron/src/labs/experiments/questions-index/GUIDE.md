# ❓ Questions index

**What it does.** A ❓ Questions tab beside Chapters / Notes that lists every question the host asked, word for word
(the analysis's `questions`; the text is `LABS.transcript.text(span)`, never AI text). Each question has a jump to
where it is asked and a **▶ answer** button that jumps to where the answer starts. A ❓ also marks each question's
paragraph in the time column. An **Interview map** mode turns the list into a numbered map of the interview that you
step through with **Q** / **Shift+Q**.

**How to try it.**
1. 🧪 Labs → switch on "Questions index"; open (or ⌘R) an analyzed video (🧪 Analyze first if the tab says
   "Not analyzed yet").
2. Click the **❓ Questions** tab. Click a question → plays from it. Click **▶ answer 3:39** → plays from the answer.
3. Type in **Filter questions…** (e.g. `AI`, `money`): only questions whose words (or answer start) begin with every
   typed word stay; matches are marked. Esc clears.
4. Click **Interview map**, click the transcript once (so keys reach the page), then press **Q** (next question,
   plays from it) and **Shift+Q** (previous; pressed more than 4 s into a question it restarts that one first).
   With a filter typed, Q / Shift+Q step through the filtered questions only. The current question is marked.

**Judge it on.** Can you skim a 2-hour interview by its questions and jump straight to the answers you care about?
Is stepping with Q a faster way to "listen to the interview's structure" than scrubbing? Are the questions really the
host's (not rhetorical questions inside answers), and does ▶ answer land at the right moment?

**Known limits.**
- Only as good as the analysis: missed or extra questions, answer starts a sentence early/late.
- Without analysis the tab only offers the 🧪 Analyze button.
- Q / Shift+Q don't work while the YouTube frame has focus (click the transcript first). The mode (list / map) is
  remembered; the filter is not.
